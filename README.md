# Privacy Policy for Sudoku Master

**Last updated:** November 4, 2026

ExamCache ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Sudoku Master mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

Sudoku Master is built with user privacy as a primary principle. We do not require account registration to play the game, and we do not collect your personal name, email address, physical location, or contact list.

### Third-Party Services
The App integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect information used to identify your device:
- **Google AdMob**: Used to display non-intrusive banner ads, post-game interstitials, and optional rewarded ad trials. AdMob may collect your device's Google Advertising ID (GAID) to serve relevant advertisements.
- **Google Play Billing**: Used to process optional one-time purchases ("Remove Ads Forever" and "Notebook & Ink Theme"). Financial transactions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your credit card or billing details.
- **Google Play Games Services (Optional)**: If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your public gamer handle and achievements/leaderboard scores are stored on Google servers.

## 2. In-App Purchases & Advertising Options
Users may permanently remove all banner and interstitial advertisements via a one-time "Remove Ads Forever" purchase.

## 3. Children's Privacy
Our App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## Firebase Crashlytics
We use Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, to find and fix crashes. When the app crashes, it may send crash details, device information such as model and OS version, and an app installation identifier. We do not use this data for advertising.

## Your advertising choices
If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before showing ads through Google AdMob. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.

Sudoku Master stores your settings, statistics and saved games only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Purchases are managed by Google Play and can be restored with the same Google account.
