# Privacy Policy for Sudoku Master

**Last updated:** November 9, 2026

ExamCache ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Sudoku Master mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

Sudoku Master is built with user privacy as a primary principle. We do not require account registration to play the game, and we do not collect your personal name, email address, physical location, or contact list.

Your games, statistics, streaks, and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive a copy.

### Third-Party Services
The App integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect information used to identify your device:
- **Google AdMob**: Used to display non-intrusive banner ads, post-game interstitials, and optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect your device's Google Advertising ID (GAID) to serve relevant advertisements.
- **Google User Messaging Platform**: In regions that require it, such as the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and remembers your choice on your device. You can change your choice at any time from "Ad Privacy Choices" in the App's Settings.
- **Firebase Crashlytics**: Used to find and fix crashes. When the App crashes, Crashlytics sends a crash report that includes technical details such as the error and where it happened in the App, your device model, operating system version, App version, and an identifier for this installation of the App. It does not include your name, email address, or game data.
- **Google Play Billing**: Used to process optional one-time purchases ("Remove Ads Forever" and "Notebook & Ink Theme"). Financial transactions and purchase restoration are handled entirely and securely by Google Play. We never receive or store your credit card or billing details.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

## 2. In-App Purchases & Advertising Options
Users may permanently remove all banner and interstitial advertisements via a one-time "Remove Ads Forever" purchase. Optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch, may still be offered. You can also reset or limit ad personalization through your device's Google advertising settings.

## 3. Children's Privacy
Our App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
